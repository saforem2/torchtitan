# MDS154391 Stage-3 teacher-free STaR experiment

Date: 2026-09-25

## Decision

The Stage-3 STaR SFT candidate is rejected for promotion and no GRPO follow-up
is released. The immutable Stage-2 model remains the accepted checkpoint.

Stage-3 improved format robustness slightly but reduced semantic accuracy on the
predeclared fixed GSM8K-200 gate:

| Model | Correct | Format-valid | Length terminations |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Stage-2 baseline | 43/200 (21.5%) | 197/200 | 3 |
| Stage-3 STaR SFT | 37/200 (18.5%) | 198/200 | 1 |

The paired comparison had 21 baseline-only correct rows and 15 candidate-only
correct rows (22 both-correct, 142 both-wrong). Exact two-sided McNemar
`p=0.4050322461`; the candidate is not significantly different and trends
worse. It fails the predeclared requirement of at least 50/200 correct with
significant improvement and no format regression.

## Phase B: bounded Stage-3 SFT

Initial job `12478714` failed during the duplicated corpus gate because it made
the same detection-vs-leakage mistake. The launcher was changed to call the
tested verifier as the single source of truth.

Replacement `12478715` ran from exact commit
`204df4538e53e1708944f9b4c2f3d06a658f5465` on two Sunspot nodes / 24 ranks:

- base: immutable Stage-2 HF checkpoint;
- accepted corpus: 4,294 self-generated rows;
- assistant-only packed SFT, max length 2,048;
- bf16 FSDP full-shard;
- LR `5e-6`, seed 42, 100 optimizer steps;
- checkpoints at 25, 50, 75, and 100.

The job completed with PBS exit zero. Loss fell from approximately `0.216` to
`0.044`, token accuracy rose from `0.920` to `0.990`, aggregate training loss
was `0.1054`, and all gradients remained finite. The predeclared final artifact
is:

`/lus/tegu/projects/datascience/foremans/reproductions/agpt2b-mds154391-broad-grain-sft900/stage3/sft-12478715/final`

These training metrics establish optimization only, not semantic quality.

## Phase C gate: semantic evaluation

Job `12478718` evaluated the exact final model on the same first 200 GSM8K test
rows, prompt serialization, greedy decoding, fp32 vLLM, 768-token limit, and
stop IDs `(1, 107)` used for Stage-2. It completed with exit zero and wrote:

`/lus/tegu/projects/datascience/foremans/reproductions/agpt2b-mds154391-broad-grain-sft900/stage3/eval-12478715-gsm8k-200/generations.jsonl`

The candidate changed behavior substantially (36 discordant correctness rows)
but did not improve it: 15 rows flipped wrong→correct while 21 flipped
correct→wrong. Because the primary semantic gate failed, full-1,319 evaluation,
IFEval, and GRPO are not warranted. Stage-2 remains the retained model.
