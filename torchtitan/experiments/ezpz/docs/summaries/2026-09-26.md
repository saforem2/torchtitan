## 3. Aurora XCCL scale gate

Aurora job `8862818` was originally summarized as `OOM`, but the detailed trace
shows a pre-training communicator failure: the first seed broadcast lazily
created XCCL communicators, many ranks failed in
`ProcessGroupXCCL::initXCCLComm` with `std::bad_alloc`, and PMI/KVS timeouts
followed as cascades. It completed zero optimizer updates.

A communicator-first ladder reproduced the real application bootstrap and
TorchTitan's XCCL `DeviceMesh` split-group workaround:
