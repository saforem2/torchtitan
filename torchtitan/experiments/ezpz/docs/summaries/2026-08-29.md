**The last two windows reverse, and that is not signal.** 5750 spans a
transient disturbance in the Mano arm; 6000 spans its rebound. Fit a crossover
through the tail and you are fitting a detour. On 2750-5500 the closure is
monotone with no reversals at all.

### What it does and does not say

**Does:** at fixed GBS=960 with per-optimizer LRs measured at that batch and
held constant, Mano reaches a lower loss than AdamW at every point past ~1.0B
tokens, by ~0.14 nats at 10B and 0.075 at 23.59B.

**Does not:** one seed per arm, and **no decay phase**. The documented prior
from earlier competitions is exactly "Mano/Muon win short runs, AdamW wins in
the cosine decay" -- so this is consistent with that prior, not a refutation.
Whether Mano's lead survives a decay phase is the obvious follow-up and is not
answered here.

### Mano's late disturbance

After 5,600 steps at 0.0%, Mano's excursion rate drifted across five windows:

| window | over 2.0 | rate | max gn | mean loss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5600 | 0 | 0.0% | 0.20 | 2.5051 |
| 5650 | 2 | 4.0% | 5.18 | 2.5226 |
| 5700 | 3 | 6.0% | 3.85 | 2.5321 |
| 5750 | 3 | 6.0% | **60.09** | 2.5319 |
| 5800 | 4 | 9.1% | 13.08 | 2.5654 |

Step 5792 was a real discontinuity -- grad_norm 0.23 -> 60.09 in one step with
loss 2.51 -> 3.03 -- and the between-spike floor rose from 0.18-0.28 to
0.6-2.7. It resolved with no further excursions after ~5850, and the arm
finished at its best loss of the run.

Recorded because the opening looks like a SophiaG regime flip and is not one.
What separates them is scale and direction: SophiaG's equivalent four windows
ran 6% -> 20% -> 74% -> 100% with mean loss 2.93 -> 4.54 and peaks in the
thousands. Mano topped out under 10%, peaked at 60, and recovered.

## 4. The `#4121` fold, continued

The token-unit migration kept surfacing. `agpt`'s attention unflatten was
committed wrong as `[B, L*N, H]`, locked in by a test, then reverted to
upstream's `[T, N, H]`; `moe` needed the same port and its MLA forward
separately; blendcorpus needed a `[B, L] -> [T]` fold; the validator and the
20B submit path needed their own token-flag ports.

Worth keeping: **a bad attention layout is a silent no-op.** `[B, L*N, H]`
makes SDPA see sequence length 1, so the output equals `v` exactly and the
model trains as a position-wise MLP. The GQA ratio check passes under it. The
only tell is `max|out - v| == 0`.

## 5. MoE trains at TP>1

> **Links:** [known-bugs page](../guides/known-bugs/moe-tp2-wo-placement.md)

The SDPA wrapper unflattened `[T,N,H] -> [B,L,N,H]` going in and never
re-flattened coming out ([`6e4e1996f`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/6e4e1996f)). MLA then reshaped against a batch
leading dim instead of a token one, and `wo` got `Shard(dim=0)` where
row-parallel wants `Partial(sum)`. `agpt` had the re-flatten all along, with a
comment warning about exactly this -- "reads the wrong stride off a 4D tensor".
The `moe` port ([`e4517ae09`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/e4517ae09)) copied the input half and dropped the output half.

Twelve hypotheses died first, and every one of them was about configuration.
Job `8789506` shows both models' SDPA wrapped on the same mesh `('tp',)(2,)`,
both declaring `['cp','dp','tp']` on every in/out/grad entry. Config
population, layout axes, arg names and the parallelize branch all match. That
is why comparing configs kept failing.

**The first fix was wrong, and only the TP=1 regression arm caught it.**
Copying `agpt`'s `out_TNH.view(out_TNH.shape[0], -1)` verbatim reads `moe`'s
BATCH axis, because `agpt`'s SDPA returns 3D and `moe`'s returned 4D:

```
RuntimeError: mat1 and mat2 shapes cannot be multiplied (1x1048576 and 2048x256)
```

That broke TP=1, which had always worked.

Verified, job `8787243`, 4 ranks, TP=2, 5 steps, nocompile:

| arm | step 1 -> 5 |
|---|---|
| `agpt` | 10.88838 -> 10.43820 |
| `moe` tp=1 | 12.93609 -> 11.32376 |
| `moe` tp=2 | 12.94930 -> 11.49335 |

The two `moe` arms agree to 0.011 at step 1, track within 0.17 over five steps,
and TP=2 holds less memory per rank than TP=1 (1.93 vs 2.78 GiB).

TP=2 only, 4 ranks, 5 steps, nocompile. No TP=4, no compiled `moe`, no scale
run, and no numeric-equivalence test -- the TP=1/TP=2 agreement is close but
not bitwise.

Five blockers came out before `moe` reached attention at all: missing
`in_dst_shardings` entries, a `dense_activation_placement()` keyword,
`Shard(n)` -> `spmd.S(n)` in the param layout, the `#4121` fold, and the
flat-MLA port. Two of the six MLA changes -- `k_pe.unsqueeze(1)` and
`expand(-1, k_nope.size(1), -1)` -- only bite at tp>1, and no single-rank test
reaches them.

## 12. Method notes worth keeping

Two errors repeated often enough this period to be worth writing down.

**Bin before characterizing.** The fresh SophiaG arm was called "healthy",
then "an isolated transient", then "a clustered regime" in three consecutive
readings, each from a handful of steps, each optimistic, each wrong. A 50-step
binned table settled it immediately. A sequence of windows is the unit of
evidence; a run of steps is not.

**Empty output from a still-running job is not a no-op.** Three `du` scans
over 13M Lustre files returned zero bytes and were diagnosed as silently
failing; `pgrep` showed all three alive. `du | sort` writes only at
completion. Establish that something FINISHED before calling it a no-op. The
actual bug, when it appeared, was a client-side `timeout` killing the ssh
while the remote command kept running -- exit 124, output lost.
