## Headline

**muP transfers at production width.** Tuning at dim 3072 and deploying at
6144 gives the same optimal learning rate (6.4e-5), verified with interior
minima on both rungs at the exact geometry the 30B runs use -- `L=64`,
`head_dim=128`, `H/dim=2.667`, `vocab=100352`. That is a 4x parameter
reduction on every future tuning run. The base rung (1536) lands one grid step
higher, so the honest claim is "transfer within one grid step across 4x, exact
across the upper 2x".

**The optimizer comparison finished, and then three of its published claims
had to be corrected.** Mano wins at 23.59B tokens (2.43889 vs AdamW's
2.51357). But "SophiaG is a clear third, 1.7 nats back" was measured entirely
after that arm's blow-up; a fresh replicate finished FIRST on loss. "Mano's
excursions are all in the late disturbance" was wrong -- 7 of 21 sit at steps
1694-1981. And "the fresh SophiaG arm escaped" held for ~2,200 steps before it
diverged a second time.

**SophiaG is 4/4 divergent, and a clean run buys no warning.** The fourth arm
was an independent from-scratch run with the RNG pinned. It ran 1,529 steps at
0.0% excursions -- nine consecutive clean windows -- then blew up. The original
arm had run 17.9% above grad_norm 2.0 for a thousand steps before ITS onset, so
the noisy prelude that looked like a precursor was lineage-specific. There is
no quiet streak long enough to certify a SophiaG run.

**Muon's headline LR is not Muon's.** At 30B it suggests 5.68e-04, 18.6x
AdamW's. Turning off its 15.677x internal rescale moved that to 5.09e-04 -- a
factor of 1.12. The AdamW-path 78.5% of parameters anchors the curve; only
21.5% are on the Muon path at all.

## 1. The optimizer comparison, final

Both healthy arms ran to the `training.steps=6000` ceiling: 23.59B tokens,
`rc=0`, 0 NaN/inf, 0 grad skips.

| arm | final loss | excursions (grad_norm > 2.0) | max |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| AdamW | 2.51357 | **0 in 5,747 steps** | 1.20 |
| Mano | **2.43889** | 21 (0.4%) | 60.09 |

The gap narrows monotonically from -0.1378 (window 2750) to -0.0793 (5500),
about 0.0187 nats per 1,000 steps, implying a crossover near 40B. The last two
windows REVERSE, but that is a transient in the Mano arm and its rebound --
fitting a crossover through the tail fits a detour.

Scope, unchanged: one seed per arm, no decay phase. The documented prior is
"Mano/Muon win short runs, AdamW wins in the cosine decay", so this is
consistent with that prior rather than a refutation.

### Three corrections

**"SophiaG is a clear third (1.7 nats back)"** came from the original arm,
which blew up at step 1048 -- every point after that measured SophiaG after a
blow-up. The fresh replicate leads at every matched step:

| step | AdamW | Mano | SophiaG fresh |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3000 | 2.81825 | 2.67857 | **2.59921** |
| 5080 | 2.60621 | 2.51114 | **2.43156** |

It did that having diverged twice and lost ~900 steps. Loss and stability are
separate results and the document had been running them together.

**"Mano's 21 excursions are all in the late disturbance"** -- 14 are, at steps
5693-5980, but 7 sit at 1694-1981 including the 3.84 spike the regime-flip
section already analysed. The 0.4% rate stands; the word "all" did not.

**"The fresh SophiaG arm escaped"** was a fair observation and a wrong
conclusion. It held the good state ~2,200 steps and reached loss 2.4984 --
below AdamW's final -- then diverged again at ~4106.

### Reconciling the two excursion censuses

The document carried "AdamW 0 in 5,747" in one section and "AdamW 54 (2.7%)"
in another. Both count the same events: unfiltered from step 21 the census
gives AdamW 54 and Mano 142, but all 54 of AdamW's sit at steps 21-161 with
`loss >= 5.0` -- ordinary early training from a fresh init. The filtered
figures (`loss < 5.0`) are the comparable ones.

## 2. SophiaG: 4/4, no precursor, escape, relapse

The fresh seeded replicate produced the complete arc in one arm:

1. **1,529 steps at 0.0% excursions**, max 0.31
2. **onset at ~1550**, escalating `0.20 -> 4.95 -> 36.1 -> 99.0 -> 731.8` in
   four steps
3. **a FALSE recovery** at window 1650 (4.0%, max 13.49) that reversed
4. deterioration to **100% of steps** above 2.0, peak **16,532**, loss 4.54
5. **a real escape** by window 1900, loss recovering below pre-onset
6. **a SECOND onset at ~4106**, after ~2,200 clean steps

Telling the two lulls apart matters, because the first fooled this project's
docs twice. The fake was 4.0% with max 13.49 -- still spiking, just less often.
The real one is 0.0% with peaks collapsing 176 -> 0.97 -> 0.23 -> 0.19 and loss
monotonically recovering. **Rate alone does not separate them.**

Severity is stochastic too: the same arm's two onsets peaked at 16,532 and 177,
with worst-window rates of 100% and 16.7%.

### The low-LR arm

A fifth arm at **half the suggested LR** (1.78e-5, seed pinned) cleared all
four prior onset steps: 1,052 in-window steps, **zero** excursions, loss 2.879
at step 1579.

**This does not yet show that lower LR helps.** At every matched step the
full-LR arm was equally clean -- 0 excursions in 574 in-window steps below
1081 -- and diverged anyway at ~1550. The loss cost of halving is near zero
(2.879 vs 2.910), so if the trade worked it would be nearly free, but the
comparison is indeterminate rather than favourable. Settling it needs ~4200
steps, past where the fresh arm's second onset fired.

## 3. muP: audit to measured transfer

Full writeup: [experiments/mup/README.md](../experiments/mup/README.md).

**The audit found the starting position unusually good.** Three of four init
groups already satisfy muP by accident -- agpt's `sqrt(2/(5*dim))` base is
exactly `fan_in^-1/2`, the embedding init is width-independent, norms are
`ones_`. Only the readout was off-spec.

**Two runtime corrections to the audit's premises.** `w3` IS depth-scaled (it
shares `w2`'s initializer through `make_ffn_config`), and agpt does not use
llama3's `0.02` base at all -- it has a complete parallel init copy.

**The coordinate check passed after the readout fight.** The literal Table 3
prescription is `d^-1` for the readout; that gave `lm_head` slope -0.130 while
every hidden module sat near zero. Scaling its LR to `eta/m` to "complete"
Table 3 made it **worse** (-0.483) -- and that was the informative result,
because a negative slope means over-scaled DOWN. Keeping agpt's existing
`fan_in^-1/2` with an O(1) readout LR gives 0.001 and passes at max `|slope|`
0.020 against a 0.05 tolerance.

**Stage 4 measured the payoff.** Three grids:

| eta | 1536 | 3072 | 6144 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1.6e-5 | 7.322 | 6.544 | 6.176 |
| **6.4e-5** | 5.877 | **5.510** | **5.520** |
| **2.56e-4** | **5.672** | 6.022 | 5.952 |
| 1.024e-3 | 6.390 | 7.084 | 6.624 |

The two upper rungs agree exactly; 1536 is one step off, which is what muP
working asymptotically looks like.

**Both earlier grids produced confident wrong verdicts at rc=0**, and the gates
written from them are the durable output:

- Grid 1 reused the tiny ladder's LR range (6 layers vs this ladder's 64), all
  three widths pinned to the left edge, and the harness called it TRANSFER --
  three identical artifacts.
- A rehearsal swept `--optimizer.param-groups.0...lr`, which moved only the
  embedding group's ONE parameter while the 42-parameter hidden group stayed
  at default. Loss varied 0.006 nats across a 16x span; the harness reported
  NO TRANSFER from noise.

Both are gated now, each tested against the real data that fooled it plus a
synthetic interior-minimum case that must still pass.

**Three integration gaps, each found by running rather than reading.** The
harness never referenced the muP builders, so it could only measure SP. The
muP flavors had no `--config` entry point, so nothing could train them. And
`MUP_ETA` had to be threaded through because tyro overrides one param group at
a time while muP needs a ratio.

## 4. Muon at 30B

First time Muon has run at this scale.
[Writeup](../experiments/lr-finder/agpt/2026-08-30-30b-gbs960-muon.md).

| arm | suggested | blow-up | min loss |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AdamW | 3.05e-05 | 3.05e-04 | **8.8381** |
| Mano | 5.61e-05 | 5.61e-04 | 8.9925 |
| SophiaG | 3.55e-05 | 3.55e-04 | 9.3441 |
| **Muon** | **5.68e-04** | **5.68e-03** | 8.8883 |

**The number is two numbers.** Muon partitions by tensor SHAPE
(`p.ndim == 2 and max(p.shape) <= 10000`), so at 30B only the attention
projections -- 21.5% of parameters -- are on its path; `w1/w2/w3` (16384) and
the embedding/head (100352) fall through to an internal AdamW branch. Muon-path
tensors then get a uniform 15.677x LR rescale. At the suggestion that is
8.90e-03 on 21.5% of params and 5.68e-04 on the rest.

**A no-rescale arm settled which population sets the optimum.** With
`adjuster_lr_ref=False` the suggestion moved to 5.09e-04 -- a factor of 1.12.
Removing a 15.677x multiplier changed the answer by 12%, so the AdamW-path
majority anchors the curve. The two curves are genuinely different (0.04-0.10
nats apart at every matched LR), so this is not a flag that failed to apply.

A prediction written into the doc before the first sweep -- that Muon's base
would land ~15x LOWER -- was refuted by a comparable factor in the other
direction. It stays in the file; being wrong about the direction is the
informative part.

## 5. Tooling failures worth recording

Every one of these failed by producing a plausible artifact at exit 0.

- **The W&B report omitted two entire arms.** `sophiag-fresh-seed1234` (10
  runs) and `sophiag-lowlr-seed1234` (3 runs) were in wandb, collected by
  `_discover`, displayed by nothing -- including the arm that finished first on
  loss. `ARM_GROUPS` enumerated three names; variants are now matched by
  prefix.
- **The LR-finder plotter would have dropped the Muon arm.** It kept its own
  hardcoded three-arm list, so registering a config and a PBS arm did not reach
  the chart. Its zoom panel also pinned `xlim(1e-5, 3e-3)`, which would have
  clipped a low Muon minimum off the left edge. Third instance of this pattern.
- **99 of ~100 repo-root `.pbs` scripts carry a dead CLI flag** on a live line
  and would abort today. Documented rather than mass-fixed:
  [known-bugs/dead-cli-flags-in-repo-root-pbs.md](../guides/known-bugs/dead-cli-flags-in-repo-root-pbs.md).
- **`lrfind_opt.pbs` was stale for a week** with `--dataloader.num-workers`,
  and would have aborted any arm, not just the new one. Caught by a preflight
  that parses the flag list extracted FROM the script rather than a retyped
  copy.
- **6.45 TB of core dumps** -- 2,192 files, larger than every checkpoint owned,
  35% of the project quota. Combined with a checkpoint prune, 8.4 TB reclaimed.
