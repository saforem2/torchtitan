## Headline

**The 30B went from a config that did not exist to a converged,
checkpoint-verified model in eight days**: four chained jobs ran a single
trajectory to step **2000/2000**, loss **12.02818 -> 2.11536**, zero NaN in any
logged loss or grad_norm, ~28% MFU held start to finish. The week's second
result is less comfortable. **Production was applying NaN gradients** -- the

eval published after those chains' RoPE switch was measured on a wrongly
permuted export**, which inverted a "monotonic ARC-C decay" that does not
exist. Nearly every 30B commit this week corrects an earlier 30B commit, and
the two biggest caveats the campaign carried were both retired by measurement
rather than by a fix.

## 3. The 30B config was decided by measurement, and the proposal lost most of it

### 3.1 Tokenizer: the custom 64k lost, OLMo-2 won

[exp07](../production/agpt/30b-exp/exp07-custom-tokenizer-feasibility.md) set
its decision rule in advance (>=8-10% better fertility = retokenize; <5% = do
not) and then failed it. A byte-level BPE trained on a 705 MB stratified sample
of olmo-mix-1124, measured on 176 MB of held-out text from disjoint shards:

| tokenizer | corpus-weighted tok/MB | vocab |
|---|---:|---:|
| custom 64k | 239,695 | 64,000 |
| gemma | 233,944 | 256,128 |
| **OLMo-2** | **225,749** | **100,278** |
| Llama-3.1 | 225,539 | 128,256 |

The custom 64k is **2.5% worse than gemma and about 6% worse than Llama-3**.
(exp07 reports the second figure as 5.9%; that uses the custom tokenizer as
denominator while its gemma comparison uses gemma's --
`(239695-225539)/225539 = 6.28%`. The doc's two headline percentages are
computed inconsistently.) A 32k candidate is worse still (+7.8%). The custom
tokenizer beats gemma on 4 of 7 domains -- and wins outright on only 2 in the
three-way table -- and still loses, because it loses on dclm, **94.8% of the
corpus**.

OLMo-2 ties Llama-3.1 (+0.09%) with a 22% smaller vocab. Confirmed on hardware
in one four-arm job (`12473207`): at LBS=4 both give **28.94% MFU to the
digit**, with OLMo-2 at 43.58 GiB (68.11%) against Llama-3's 48.62 GiB
(75.98%). That 7.9-9.0 points of freed HBM is what made LBS=5 reachable
(76.83% vs 85.84%). Config landed as `agpt_30b_olmo2tok`, vocab 100,352
([`c01f1a4a3`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/c01f1a4a3)).

The proposal's ~64k custom-tokenizer recommendation is dead, and the reason
generalizes: "train on your own data" pays only when your data is unusual, and
94.8% of ours is generic web text Llama-3 was already fit on at vastly greater
scale. exp01's mass-concentration argument for ~64k also does not survive --
rare ids are cheap to keep and expensive to drop, so fertility depends on the
tail. Fertility knee, measured: 128k->100k costs +0.09%, 100k->64k costs +6.2%,
64k->32k costs +12.4%.

### 3.2 LBS=5 is the pick, and "indistinguishable" was wrong

A ladder (job `12473208`) put LBS 5/6/7 at 498/504/507 tps and
76.83%/87.69%/94.88% memory -- flattening at the top, so 6 and 7 are out on
headroom. That flattening was then extrapolated downward to predict LBS=4 and
LBS=5 would be indistinguishable, which would have made LBS=4 the pick.
Measured interleaved (4,5,4,5,4,5), three repeats each in one job:

| arm | tps (n=3) | MFU | within-job spread |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LBS=4 | 499.3 | 28.95% | 0.2% |
| LBS=5 | **511.3** | **29.63%** | 0.4% |

+2.4% tps, +0.68pp MFU, +8.7pp memory -- about 27x the within-job spread.
Commits [`4519d5aea`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/4519d5aea),
[`1b6372a26`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/1b6372a26).

The reusable part is the noise-floor correction
([`e435ccd13`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/e435ccd13)):
within-job repeatability is ~0.1-0.4%, but **cross-job spread is ~3%**. Any
cross-job difference under ~3% is not evidence. An earlier +0.73pp version of
the LBS claim came from two different jobs and was nearly refuted by a later
cross-job run; interleaving turned an undecidable comparison into a 27-sigma
one.

**Superseded within the week:** the LR-finder work
([`51f6ac408`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/51f6ac408)) found
**LBS=5 is impossible at a pinned GBS=6144** -- `6144 = 2^11 * 3` has no factor
of 5, so `trainer.py`'s divisibility assert fails at every node count. The
admissible set under a pinned global batch is powers of two. exp07's production
LBS=5 cannot be carried into a pinned-GBS sweep.

### 3.3 Weak scaling: 1.42% decay per doubling, but 128N is the argument

[exp06](../production/agpt/30b-exp/exp06-scaling.md), job `12473198`, one job so
all six points share a stack, build and allocation: 27.45 / 27.03 / 26.53 /
26.67 / 26.36 / **25.54%** MFU at 2/4/8/16/32/64 nodes -- **93.0% retention
across a 32x scale-up, 1.42% decay per doubling against the 2B's 13.96%**. A
second ladder (`12473204`) on the better config beat the first by ~1pp at every
point and added an LBS=1 arm costing a stable 7-8pp and decaying more than
twice as fast (3.00%/doubling vs 1.39%).

The second ladder also surfaced the constraint the proposal omits:
`GAS = GBS / (LBS * dp_degree)`, so pinning global batch at the 2B's known-good
6,144 forces LBS down as nodes grow. LBS=4 is available to 128N; at 512N only
LBS=1 is.

**Extrapolated, three doublings past the last measured point** and flagged as
such in exp06: 128N at LBS=4 gives ~26.20% MFU, 512N at LBS=1 gives ~17.13%.
Against the 2B's measured 8.79% at 512N that is a **1.95x multiplier, not the
proposed 3x** -- and 128N is 1.53x better per GPU than 512N on a quarter of the
machine. Both multipliers are ratios of extrapolated values. An earlier version
of this extrapolation used only the 32->64 doubling, got 5.63%/doubling and
projected 15.8%; the full six-point fit gives 3.00% and 17.1%.

Also corrected: "global batch necessarily grows with node count." It is a
config field, and only appears to track node count because the default `-1`
falls back to `LBS * dp_degree`.

### 3.4 Checkpoint interval at 30B is a capacity decision, and it filled the disk

Job `12473509` set `--checkpoint.interval=100` on the reasoning that more
restart points are cheap. At 294 G per checkpoint, 2000/100 writes is ~5.7-5.9 T
against the free space at the time. It died with `Errno 28` during a checkpoint
write -- **not a training fault**: the log has 1067 no-space lines, and the
model had run 100 clean steps from 801 (2.71222) to 900 (2.58698) with zero
NaN. Fixed with interval=250, deliberately **not**
`--checkpoint.keep-latest-k`; deleting history to buy space trades a permanent
asset for a temporary one, and keep-latest-k stays 0. The partial step-900
directory left behind is harmless -- `_find_load_step`
(`components/checkpointer/dcp.py:640-684`) only counts step dirs holding
`.metadata` or `model.safetensors.index.json`.

### 3.6 The step-1300 bump is noise

Step 1300 of `12473515` reads 2.43408 against step 1200's 2.37702 and looks
like a regression. It is batch noise: loss oscillates in a +/-0.05 band about a
descending mean while grad_norm falls monotonically (0.2263 -> 0.1998 across

signature -- grad_norm rising.

## 4. Production was applying NaN gradients

Landing 2026-08-21 evening local (2026-08-22 UTC), and arguably the most
consequential code change of the week.
[`a3e0480d7`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/a3e0480d7),
[`87617ab0d`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/87617ab0d),
[`8960346a6`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/8960346a6),
[`8810c90af`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/8810c90af).

**The NaN guard ran after `optimizers.step()` and inspected only the loss.**
Guard at `trainer.py:1085`, step at `:871`. Our own 80B report is the proof:
job `12473142` logged `grad_norm nan @30, loss nan @31, nan-abort @35` -- the
gradient went bad a full step before the signal we were watching, and **five
more updates landed on top of it.** Job `8663177` separately ran ~370 NaN steps
unbounded.

The existing guard is weaker than it looks: `nan_abort_consecutive` defaults to
0 (off) and production omits the flag **entirely**, because the pinned
pre-#3623 clones have no such field and passing it crashes every rank (it
killed umbrella `8680578`). So at production, a NaN gradient was applied and
nothing stopped the run.

Fix: a host-side `isfinite` check on `grad_norm` immediately before the step --
non-finite means zero_grad, log, skip the step, still advance the LR scheduler.
No new collective: `grad_norm` is already rank-reduced inside
`clip_grad_norm_`, so every rank branches identically.

Deliberately **not** upstream's mechanism. #4226 fixes the same ordering bug
with `torch._assert_async`, where a failed device-side assert invalidates the
process -- our failover machinery would read that as a crash rather than a
clean stop, and its XPU behavior is undocumented. It also lands in
`Trainer.train_step`, which `FaultTolerantTrainer` overrides wholesale, so
merging it would have given us nothing. The most valuable commit in the sync
was valuable only as a hand-port.

Smoked rather than reasoned about (job `12473623`): 10/10 bit-identical against
the parent commit, guard fired 0 times, tps 337-338 vs 338-340. Stated
honestly: ~0.3% is **below** what a single 10-step comparison resolves, so
bit-identity is the result and "costs nothing" is not yet earned. And the smoke
proves the guard is inert, not that it fires -- that path still needs a
divergent config.

Same window, a tripwire for a sync we have not taken: upstream's fold-batch-dim
(#4121) reshapes the LM stack from `[B, L]` to a flat `[T]` layout. Our three
hand-written SDPA/flex wrappers take `q_BLNH` and immediately `transpose(1, 2)`;
on 3D input that swaps N with H instead of L with N, **and SDPA accepts it** --
no traceback, just a quietly degraded loss curve. An `ndim == 4` assert at each
of the three entry points converts the worst failure mode in that sync from
silent to loud. Passes today, constant-folded under dynamo. (Earlier analysis
said four sites; there are exactly three.)

## 5. The RoPE-permute artifact: the "20B ARC-C decay" does not exist

This is the largest correction of the week, and it went through four wrong
answers before landing.

**The mechanism.** `convert_to_hf.py` decides the RoPE convention from the
`--model_flavor` you pass, not from the checkpoint -- the rope cache is
`persistent=False`, so a complex-trained and a cos_sin-trained checkpoint are
byte-indistinguishable. Applying the interleaved <-> rotate_half permute to
weights that are already rotate_half-native pairs each channel with the wrong
partner. The export loads without error and shows up only as degraded quality.

**Four attempts, three of them wrong:**

| attempt | claim | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [`aadddc587`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/aadddc587) | per-chain default (one direction) | wrong |
| [`83b52a6e4`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/83b52a6e4) | per-chain default (opposite direction) | wrong |
| [`6a7a69ca2`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/6a7a69ca2) | **no default -- the chains switched mid-flight** | correct |
| [`e86d98fc8`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/e86d98fc8) | the eval scripts were the damage, not the default | correct |

The premise of the first two was wrong: there is no per-chain answer. W&B
`metadata.args` -- the literal argv each run executed -- shows the chains
changed convention when [`5ffb850a1`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/5ffb850a1)
(2026-06-25) flipped the default and each picked it up on its next resume.
`20b_v2_256` at 07-10 (loss 2.6854 -> 6.1367), `20b_v2_512` at 07-05
(2.5669 -> 6.0298), `2b_v2_512` at 08-05, `2b_v2_256` never. The right flavor
depends on **which step** is converted, so both entry points now refuse to
guess, with `scripts/eval/rope_flavor_for_step.py` resolving step -> flavor
from W&B. [`07981f8f9`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/07981f8f9)
marks the superseded reasoning in exp04 and the 08-14 summary, keeping the
wrong inference in place under a CAUTION as the concrete example of the rule it
violates: never derive a run's config from a submit script, only from what the
run recorded.

**The eval scripts were worse than the default.** `eval-20b-v2.sh:132`
hardcoded `--model_flavor "20b"`, and the pinned v2 clones ship **no**
`agpt/state_dict_adapter.py`, falling back to the bare `Llama3StateDictAdapter`
which permutes unconditionally -- so in those clones even passing `20b_real`
would still permute. The flavor flag is inert there.

**The A/B settles it.** Job `8760246`, same checkpoint, same harness, only
`--model_flavor` differs:

| metric | step 5000 delta | step 6000 delta |
|---|---:|---:|
| arc_challenge | +0.0452 | **+0.0947** |
| arc_easy | +0.0362 | +0.0362 |
| hellaswag acc_norm | +0.0469 | +0.0490 |

Only ARC-Challenge's penalty grows, and the corrected trajectory **rises** on
every task (arc_c 0.3575 -> 0.3660, arc_e 0.7050 -> 0.7151, hellaswag
0.6394 -> 0.6576). Replicated independently on 20B-256, where the corrupted
series falls (0.3481 -> 0.3234 -> 0.3217 -> 0.3319) while the corrected series
over the *same* checkpoints is flat-to-up (0.3635 -> 0.3652 -> 0.3626 ->
0.3712). Confirmed over 13 matched pairs, steps 4000-5800: published nets
**-0.0512** where corrected nets **+0.0222**. **The published trend is
inverted, not merely depressed.**

**Retracted along the way** ([`77933a3e1`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/77933a3e1),
[`739ba4bd9`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/739ba4bd9),
[`8f0b0dfce`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/8f0b0dfce)): I
argued twice, hard, that a real hard-reasoning regression was hiding behind the
conversion bug. It does not exist. The flaw is worth keeping: I claimed steps
4500-4900 were undamaged because they scored inside the complex controls'
range -- but those steps were *also* wrongly permuted. I compared corrupted
numbers to clean ones, saw overlap, and called it evidence of no damage. The
model's genuine gain across that window (~+0.04) happened to cancel the permute
penalty (~-0.045), so the boundary looked continuous. A confident argument built
on a coincidence of magnitudes, repeated twice.

**The completed 2B-512 headline numbers were affected.** Job `8760307`
re-converted step 46,429 with the correct flavor:

| metric | published | corrected | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MMLU | 0.2511 | 0.2579 | +0.007 |
| ARC-C | 0.2381 | **0.2978** | +0.060 |
| HellaSwag | 0.4753 | **0.5384** | +0.063 |

Same asymmetry as the 20B: corruption costs most on tasks the model actually
learned, and barely touches one it never learned. **The MMLU conclusion in
30b-exp 1.1 is unchanged** and now rests on a corrected number -- 0.2579 is
still inside noise of the 0.25 floor. But the token-matched control needed the
biggest fix: it cited 2B-256 beating 2B-512 on fewer tokens (8.4pp HS, 9.2pp
ARC-C); corrected, three quarters of that gap was the permute (2.1pp, 3.2pp),
and the residual needs no bug -- 2B-256 runs a 2x smaller global batch at the
same token count, an already-measured per-token advantage.
[`e3cff11bd`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/e3cff11bd).

**Ancillary:** the eval plotters' MDS tokens/step constant was wrong by +10.3%

## 6. The 2026-08-21 umbrella seat audit

One agent per seat plus three cross-cutting agents, each finding adversarially
re-verified against the artifacts on disk. **All five seat audits came back
`audit-needs-correction`**, including on the most important finding. Full
record: [20260821-umbrella-seat-audit.md](../experiments/agpt/aurora/20260821-umbrella-seat-audit.md).

### 6.1 The t3 RoPE defect: a silent corruption caught one commit later

[`efcae5419`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/efcae5419) replaced
the umbrella's single global `CONFIG_SUFFIX="${CONFIG_SUFFIX-_real}"` -- applied
identically to all five seats -- with a required 12th TRAINERS field carrying
the RoPE flavor per seat, plus a fail-loud guard testing for the key being
**unset** (not empty, since empty is the meaningful value for `complex`) and
rejecting anything that is not `_real|complex`, `exit 95`.

But it over-corrected t3, setting field 12 to `complex` by resolving the flavor
against the *parent* chain at the *seed* step.
[`614eaeaa7`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/614eaeaa7)
corrected it to `_real`: every t3 run in history launched `--config=agpt_2b_real`
(grep of the trainer consoles in umbrellas `8756957` and `8764675` returns
`agpt_2b_real` and nothing else), and the fork's own step-21300 was written
2026-08-17, long after the 2026-06-25 switch. The rule now written into the
script: for a fork with an empty field 8, resolve RoPE against the flavor that
wrote the **fork's own latest checkpoint**, not the parent's at the branch point.

**Nothing was corrupted.** The bad value existed only between the two commits,
and t3 never launched under it -- it has zero metric lines in all four
umbrellas in this window. The exposure was the *queued* successor, below.

### 6.2 `8769730` replaced by `8773440` on a 61-second margin

PBS snapshots the submit script at qsub. `8769730`'s ctime is
**2026-08-20 21:55:44 UTC**; `efcae5419` was committed at **21:54:43 UTC**
(16:54:43 -0500). Sixty-one seconds. PBS does not expose the snapshotted bytes,
so whether it captured the `complex` t3 row is **inferred from the margin
alone, never read directly**. Its dependency had already finished, so it was
waiting only on nodes and could have started at any moment. Rather than bet a
122-checkpoint chain on that, `8773440` (same 2098 nodes, `large` queue, ctime
2026-08-21 20:01:08 UTC, from
[`ee80b89b9`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/ee80b89b9)) was
submitted and confirmed `Q` **before** `qdel 8769730`, so no window existed
with no successor queued.

Second instance of the same trap in one window: the 20B constant-LR fix
[`b08fccfd1`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/b08fccfd1) (Aug 19
17:23 UTC) never reached job `8764675` (ctime Aug 18 20:03 UTC), so `8764675`
trained a full 12h cycle on the decaying config -- confirmed in its launch line,
which points at the canonical dir with `--training.steps=46429` and no
decay-ratio flag. The lesson recorded: check `qstat -f <id> | grep ctime`
against the fix-commit date whenever a script fix lands with jobs already
queued.

### 6.3 t4 retired: the "step-9500" seed was never a trained checkpoint

The 2B-256 constant-LR seat produced zero real training steps across every
umbrella it was seated in. Forensics on its seed found near-random-init weights
mislabeled step-9500. Two lines of evidence are independently confirmed:

- **Size and dtype**, measured on disk: the seed is 13,019,268,752 B (bf16)
  where the known-good step-92859 is 24,939,666,523 B (fp32-only) -- 1.92x, not
  the "exactly 2x" the doc states.
- **Behavior**, from the smoke console: job `8772046` (4 trainer nodes in a
  5-node allocation, 8 steps) opens at step 1 loss **5.97244** / grad_norm
  0.7757 and is flat through step 8 at 5.94729. That is pretraining from
  scratch at a fine-tuning LR, not a resume.

The remaining forensics in the audit doc -- per-tensor statistics
(`attention_norm.weight` exactly 1.0 with std 0.0; `tok_embeddings` std
0.99999; absmax exactly 2.0000) and cosine-to-parent near zero on wq/wk/wv/head
-- are **doc-sourced**; no script producing them was committed this week, so I
could not re-derive them. The retirement stands on the dtype evidence plus the
flat smoke.

**One probe in the audit is void and should not be republished.** The doc, the
script comment, and `614eaeaa7`'s message all claim the seed's
`ntokens_seen = 155,648,000` is "325x too few" against the ~478e9 implied by
step 9500. The true ratio is exactly **3072** -- which is the rank count
(256 nodes x 12 ranks). `torchtitan/trainer.py:763` accumulates
`self.ntokens_seen += labels.numel()` **per rank**, and `state_dict()` persists
that un-reduced value; only the logged metric is all-reduced. Local batch is
6144/3072 = 2, so `9500 * 2 * 8192 = 155,648,000` exactly. The counter is
consistent with a genuine step-9500 and is evidence of nothing. It survived to
publication in three places -- a sharper illustration of the audit's own theme
than the theme statement makes.

The retirement also closes a chain of misattribution. t4's death was blamed in
turn on the qkv_linear rename, then the `output.weight` -> `lm_head.weight`
rename, then a stale clone of the shim, then the pre-#3623 nested->flat
optimizer format. Each was a real defect that had to be fixed to reach the
next; none was the root cause.

### 6.4 t4 replaced by a 256N stage-2 dolmino arm, smoke-validated at 2.591

Redefined as the 256N twin of t0, seeded weights-only from the completed 256N
stage-1 chain at **step-92859** -- the last step under budget
(92,859 x 6144 x 8192 = 4,673,746,501,632 against a 4,673,780,159,710 target;
step 92,860 would exceed it). `rope=complex` was verified rather than assumed:
that chain never crossed the switch.

Smoke `8773381` (4 trainer nodes, 8 steps) confirmed it against pre-registered
criteria (~2.6-2.7 = correct flavor and good seed; ~7.2 = flavor still wrong).
From the trainer console: `--config=agpt_2b`, step 1 loss **2.59128** /
grad_norm 0.6761, validate step 1 loss 2.5527, step 8 loss 2.59126 -- matching
the 512N twin's 2.60254 resume.

Token budget is deliberately the **same** 2,390,375,382,006 as t0 rather than
halved: `steps = tokens / (gbs * seq_len)`, so the same value yields 47,492
steps at gbs 6144 and 23,746 at gbs 12288 -- **291,790,848 samples either way**,
which is what makes the arms comparable. (The dispatch-log's "23,742" at line
145 is a typo.)

### 6.5 Two "corrupt checkpoint" mysteries resolved as one cause

- **"step-9200 resumes at ~6.5"**, recorded as a corrupt checkpoint. It is not.
  Job `8744247` logged step 9201 loss 6.51705 / grad_norm 18.4066 and
  re-converged to 2.93 by step 9300 -- the one-time complex-under-cos_sin
  transition cost at the fork's first resume. step-9200 is fp32 with cosine
  0.999 to its parent. (Job aged out of PBS; figures are doc-sourced. I did
  confirm step-9200 is 24G with 6144 shards, consistent with fp32.)
- **The 2026-07-18 stage-2 NaN** (job `8663177`), blamed on the
  olmo50/dolmino50 mix or the 2e-6 LR. Neither. It launched the
  complex-trained step-92859 seed under `agpt_2b_real` and resumed at loss
  7.23840 / grad_norm 46.4271, where the correct-flavor 512N twin resumes at
  2.60254 / 0.2505. It spent 3,800 steps re-learning the scrambled Q/K pairing
  back to 2.63, then NaN'd. The 512N seat runs pure dolmino at roughly 10x the
  LR and is clean past step 7,700.

Both were logged as data or checkpoint faults; both were the same configuration
defect. The practical consequence is that the recipe was never the problem,
which is what licenses the new 256N arm. A flavor mismatch now has a recognized
signature: clean load, resume loss ~7.2 vs ~2.6, grad_norm ~46 vs ~0.25.

### 6.6 t1 and t0: fixed operationally, not in code

**t1** (20B-512 constant-LR fork from step-9000) had never actually run -- the
healthy step 10600 previously attributed to it was the **canonical** chain, and
the fork dir did not exist. Its field 11 is the cumulative stage-1 target, which
is correct only under a **pre-copy** bootstrap (the counter continues from
9000); seeding model-only restarts the counter and adds 46,429 steps on top of
the 9,000 already done -- a **19.4% overshoot**, same bug class as the t0 fix in
[`cc4e22cfa`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/cc4e22cfa).
Resolution: pre-copy step-9000, which also preserves the SophiaG optimizer state
a model-only seed discards. Verified byte-level and re-verified independently on
disk: 6144 shards both sides, `.metadata` byte-exact at **1,252,226,661**, and a
`du -sb` delta of exactly **20,480 B = 5 x 4096** of directory inode overhead.
[`215c68379`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/215c68379).

**t0** was blocked on a cold blendcorpus index cache. The cache key is an md5
over a descriptor including `Number of samples`, derived from `training.steps`,
so `cc4e22cfa`'s **correct** budget fix (70,176 -> 23,746 steps, 2.9553x) made
84G of Aug-16 cache permanently unreachable. Pre-warmed (job `8773399`):
corpus-level entries went 30 -> 42, each corpus building in **under a second**.
The job then crashed on an Intel GPU fault at "Training starts at step 1" --
after "blendcorpus datasets ready" -- so the artifact was complete before the
fault; verified post-crash at 21 corpus entries, 0 incomplete.

Two things worth keeping. The sub-second corpus builds **undercut** the theory
that a cold corpus build is what times out the 60 s CCL KVS barrier at 6144
ranks, so that failure mode stays open. And: **symlink the directory, not its
contents** -- 3,933 per-file symlinks created for the new 256N seat before the
prewarm would have hidden exactly the 18 entries the prewarm then wrote, and the
failure would have looked like a cold cache rather than a stale link set.

## 8. Checkpoint-format compatibility, and the tooling that made the smokes cheap

`ckpt_key_compat` remaps pre-refactor checkpoint keys at `dcp.load` time. Three
renames now compose in one wrapper:

- the attention `qkv_linear` wrapper;
- `output.weight` -> `lm_head.weight`
  ([`e1320edf9`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/e1320edf9)),
  anchored in [`b3253ad96`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/b3253ad96)
  so the regex cannot hit `layers.N.moe.output.weight`;
- the **optimizer** half
  ([`40ffc9215`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/40ffc9215)),
  which the shim had never seen -- torchtitan expands only the MODEL to flat
  FQNs, so `_down("optimizer")` was a guaranteed no-op and the real optimizer
  FQNs appear only *inside* `dcp.load`. The fix scope-swaps the container's
  `state_dict()` for the duration of the load and restores it in a `finally`,
  because the save path also calls `state_dict()` and a permanent wrapper would
  write **new** checkpoints in the **old** spelling -- turning a read shim into
  a corruption source.

[`2e24fdbe9`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/2e24fdbe9) then
fixed a structural blind spot: the detector probed only `checkpoint.folder`, but
`initial_load_path` is honored **only** when that folder holds no resumable
step, so a seeded fresh chain is by construction the case the shim could not
see. [`de1328cf7`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/de1328cf7)
fixed a test that self-shadowed by importing the pinned clone's older module
ahead of the one under test.

**The shim is exonerated for t4 and not certified.** A key rename cannot change
a dtype, and step-9200 -- which does not go through the remap -- is healthy, so
exactly one of the two suspect checkpoints was a remap consumer. But
`ckpt_key_compat` has still never been validated end-to-end on a known-good
checkpoint. The standing hazard is unchanged: **every** production 2B/20B
checkpoint on disk still spells the head `output.weight`, and the live chains
avoid the break only because their clones are pinned to pre-rename code.

**MULTI_ONLY / MULTI_NNODES_OVERRIDE / MULTI_STEPS_OVERRIDE.** Three earlier
attempts to test the t4 seed hand-built the `ezpz launch` invocation and burned
an allocation each: a missing venv activation; `--optimizer.name sophiag` where
this build wants `--optimizer=sophiag`; then "Optimizer SophiaG not added"
because `ezpz launch` needs `--nproc`/`--nproc_per_node`/`--hostfile` and a `--`
separator and must run from the node-local venv. Each fix revealed the next --
the signature of reconstructing the command *shape* rather than of any one wrong
flag.
[`1d646a91b`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/1d646a91b) added
`MULTI_ONLY` (comma-separated seat indices, filtered **before** the node-budget
loop so `need`, slicing and offsets all see only the selected seats, and
rejecting an out-of-range index rather than silently selecting nothing) plus
`MULTI_NNODES_OVERRIDE`. Unlike `MULTI_PROFILE=tiny`, `MULTI_ONLY` keeps each
seat's real ckpt dir, config, dataset and RoPE flavor. (Note
[`af0863575`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/af0863575)'s own
message misattributes these to `d62d1ece2`, which git confirms is a merge
commit of `1d646a91b` and `42a6e4b4c`.)

Attempt 4 (`8771637`) exposed a further trap: `--checkpoint.initial-load-path`
**did** reach the trainer, and torchtitan declined to use it -- `initial_load_path`
seeds a fresh chain and never overrides a resumable checkpoint already in
`checkpoint.folder` (`checkpoint.py:686`), so the smoke re-tested the very
checkpoint it meant to bypass.
[`c2131f3f2`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/c2131f3f2) fixed it
by rewriting the ckpt-dir field to empty scratch as well, and added
`MULTI_STEPS_OVERRIDE` because a prod seat derives `training.steps` from
`train_tokens` (attempt 4 launched with 5,943,018 steps).

**Retracted:** an earlier revision claimed the flag never reached the trainer.
Wrong -- it reached the trainer intact and torchtitan declined it. The
hypothesis was right; the evidence cited for it was not
([`6f3ad990b`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/6f3ad990b)).
Related process lesson: grep
`logs/multi-autoretry-<jobid>/trainer-N-*.console.log`, not the umbrella's `.o`
file, which carries only the wrapper's stdout.

## 9. Data provenance became its own discipline

Three audit tools were written this week and each found real damage.

- **Unregistered W&B runs**
  ([`07d4d548f`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/07d4d548f), new
  page [unregistered-wandb-runs.md](../guides/known-bugs/unregistered-wandb-runs.md)).
  A missing run-id in `trajectories.py` is invisible: the chain still plots,
  still shows a live state, it just silently drops every step that run logged.
  Four wrong outputs this month traced to it, all found by accident. The tool
  asks W&B directly -- `metadata.args` records the literal argv, so
  `--checkpoint.folder` says authoritatively which chain a run belongs to.
  First real use ([`777941c64`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/777941c64))
  flagged 29 unregistered runs of which **8 were genuine gaps and 21 were not**,
  and that distinction is the whole point: `20b_v2_256` had been under-reporting
  its own progress by ~25% (registered head 8,333 while training had reached
  10,380). The worst single case
  ([`add35be54`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/add35be54)):
  `ud8t6d3t`, the run that carried the flagship its last 2,611 steps to the
  4.674T target and the **only** non-crashed run in the entire chain, was never
  listed. The symptom did not look like a gap -- every doc said COMPLETE, the
  plot correctly showed data ending at 43,818 -- so it read as a rendering quirk
  until the user noticed the curve did not reach the right edge.
- **Run-id ORDER is semantically load-bearing**
  ([`92889b65d`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/92889b65d),
  [`bddd7a60a`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/bddd7a60a),
  [`8fdf341e6`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/8fdf341e6)), with
  [`2626297ce`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/2626297ce) auditing
  it. `olog_wins`
  ([`28460d278`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/28460d278)) adds
  the case the union merge had hardcoded wrong: when two jobs both seat and
  train the same steps concurrently, W&B and the `.o` log are *different
  trajectories*, and only checkpoint mtimes settle which one owns the weights.
  Measured on `20b_v2_256`, 2026-07-10: over the 3401..3602 overlap the two
  sources differ by up to 0.0415 and the sign flips. Default is a strict no-op.
- **Two-writer checkpoint collisions**
  ([`29a3a4e39`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/29a3a4e39) audits,
  [`bae88a1f6`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/bae88a1f6) claims
  the dir at runtime so a collision is loud). The audit's verdict on
  `20b_v2_256`: **no production checkpoint the chain actually uses is corrupt**,
  16 resume events traced, but two directories physically hold shards from two
  jobs at two rank counts, and the 256-node version of both is permanently
  destroyed -- ~560 GB of dead bytes, quarantined by a rename with **no
  identified source in the repo**. Luck, not design.

Roughly 15,500 previously-unreported steps were recovered across the 2B and 20B
chains via these tools and `.o`-log backfill (the flagship's 9,117-step hole was
two fully-synced runs simply absent from the list; the 20B-256 gaps I twice
called "permanent" were a search failure -- the logs live in the **main repo's**
umbrella log dirs, not the per-model clone). A new committed
[metrics store](../production/metrics/README.md) turns the trajectory data into
a repo artifact: 1.69 MB summary in-tree (every `olog` row at full fidelity plus
a per-chain step-stride backbone), 32.44 MB full store on flare, so plots and
dashboards stop re-pulling ~92 W&B runs.

## 10. Platform findings

**XPU determinism, characterized in four passes -- three of them corrections.**
"needs multi-rank AND seq>=4096"
([`1b57373b3`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/1b57373b3)) ->
"INTER-NODE, not multi-rank"
([`a76f8e9a7`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/a76f8e9a7)) ->
final grid "multi-NODE + seq>=2560"
([`0305305a3`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/0305305a3)) ->
re-measured on the 2.14 nightly
([`ad4cd986e`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/ad4cd986e)). The
terminal finding
([`6f1a09819`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/6f1a09819), job
`12473503`): **it is a bf16 precision artifact, and fp32 compute removes it.**

| cell | control | spmd vs partial |
|---|---|---|
| 1 node, bf16 | 10/10 | bit-identical |
| 1 node, fp32 | 10/10 | bit-identical |
| 2 nodes, **bf16** | **0/10**, diverges step 1 | -- |
| 2 nodes, **fp32** | **10/10** | **bit-identical** |

agpt was already fp32 for master weights and for the reduce; the gap was
`mixed_precision_param`, which defaults to bfloat16. Recipe for any future
numerics comparison on this stack: add
`--training.mixed-precision-param=float32`, which turns a 0/10 control into
10/10. Diagnostic only -- roughly doubles activation memory. A prior claim of
mine, that the 2N divergence was a loss-reduction effect because grad_norm was
identical, held for the one cell measured and does **not** generalize (at
seq2048 grad_norm differs too: 6.1255 vs 6.1249). Also corrected: seq=2048 is
not universally safe, and `--debug.deterministic` costs ~27% device memory on
MoE.

**SPMD backends.** Upstream #4085 flipped the default to `spmd_types` and
renamed `"default"` to `partial_dtensor`; #4217 is deleting `full_dtensor`. New
[status page](../guides/spmd-backend-status.md). `spmd_types` fails on our
shipped build for a **PyTorch-side** reason, not a torchtitan one. Two
retractions here:
[`60a3e4691`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/60a3e4691) --
I wrote up the TP>1 TypeError as a second upstream bug "confirmed on
upstream/main"; it is **our config gap**, `CrossEntropyLoss.Config` exposes
`global_vocab_size` with a docstring naming this exact use case and an upstream
unit test covering it, and I verified no caller passes it without checking
whether the config exposes it. And
[`7b01f79b6`](https://github.com/saforem2/torchtitan/commit/7b01f79b6) -- I then
claimed a "third, unidentified failure" on the nightly, from two independent
errors: reading UTC `.o` mtimes as CDT (so post-fix jobs looked pre-fix), and a
grep scoped to one failure mode. **Every** failing TP>1 arm dumps
`"global_vocab_size": null`; no failing arm ever ran with the fix. The
instrument, again: ask the run's dumped config, not the clock. Post-fix
coverage is bit-identical at TP=2 and TP=4, one node and two, dense and MoE,
three sequence lengths; all three AC modes run backend-independently with memory

[intel-ur-die-urEventWait-a2a.md](../upstream-issues/intel-ur-die-urEventWait-a2a.md).
