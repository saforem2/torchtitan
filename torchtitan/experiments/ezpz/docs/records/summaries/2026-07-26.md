## 1. The RL / chain-of-thought campaign (the biggest theme)

A multi-week arc to take agpt-2b from "has seen math rationales" to "emits and
benefits from an explicit `<think>` scratchpad," run as the DeepSeek-R1 recipe
2B-sized: cold-start CoT-SFT first, then GRPO-RLVR, on our owned XPU-validated
stacks. Verdict after Stages 0-2 ([`rl/plans/cot.md`](../../production/rl/plans/cot.md)):

- **Structure, not mix, is the SFT lever.** B2 two-stage SFT
  (tulu-math -> gsm8k-r1cot) hit **GSM8K 0.205**, beating every single-stage
  rebuild (B3 0.05, B4b 0.065) regardless of mix/filter/seqlen; ~0.2 is the 2B
  ceiling. Finishing on a verbose base can't be un-taught (B4a 0.02).
- **GRPO is drift-proof but not the accuracy lever at 2B.** On the strong B2
  cold-start, gated GRPO perfects format (0.985 -> 1.0, no reward-hack) but
  accuracy stays flat (200-problem GSM8K 0.205 -> 0.215 = noise). At ~20% GSM8K
  the correct-rollout density is too thin for RL to bite; accuracy work moves
  upstream to the cold-start SFT.
- **Reward-hacking is real and eval != reward.** An earlier Stage-2 run
  *regressed* eval (format 0.955 -> 0.635, acc 0.16 -> 0.105) while reward
  *rose*, from down-weighting the SFT-taught format guard -- always eval a real
  checkpoint, never trust reward-as-proxy.
- **vLLM `reasoning_content` split** silently zeroed a CoT-format reward: vLLM's
  DefaultRenderer strips `<think>` out of `content` into `reasoning_content`
  (tags gone); the reward now reads both.

## 2. Ceiling-attack -- reward shape beats the plateau

The agpt-2b alphabet_sort GRPO reward plateaus at ~0.25 and **no** tuning lever
(lr/rank/batch) beats it -- it is the reward *shape*, not model capacity. A
**componentized additive reward** (format + completeness + order) hit **+168%**
on the *identical* cross-scored original metric. The operating rule that came out
of it: always re-score a shaped reward on the shared original metric to prove a
real gain, not just a higher shaped number. This result is the direct input to
the CoT GRPO reward design. [`rl/grpo/ceiling-attack.md`](../../production/rl/grpo/ceiling-attack.md).

## 3. GRPO+LoRA on XPU -- reproduced, vendored, ported

- **Upstream repro**: the Monarch + TorchStore + vLLM GRPO+LoRA example
  reproduced end-to-end on XPU (train steps fire ~3270 tok/s). The real blocker
  was `torch.accelerator.get_memory_info` returning free=0 on XPU; fix =
  `torch.xpu.mem_get_info`. [`rl/history/grpo-lora-xpu-repro.md`](../../production/rl/history/grpo-lora-xpu-repro.md).
- **Vendored into `ezpz`** as a thin overlay driving current-upstream
  `experiments/rl` with `ezpz.agpt` -- zero edits to `experiments/rl` or core;
  runs from the repo ROOT (the venv's editable torchtitan maps to the fork).
- **Ported to agpt-2b** (Llama) on an SFT checkpoint: gibberish root cause was
  bf16 in the vLLM path (bare-vLLM bf16 = gibberish, fp32 = coherent); fix =
  `--generator.model-dtype=float32`. Low reward is out-of-distribution (the ckpt
  was SFT'd on tulu/math, not alphabet-sort), not a bug.
  [`rl/history/grpo-lora-agpt2b-repro.md`](../../production/rl/history/grpo-lora-agpt2b-repro.md).
- **Monarch** is the working path for the CoT loop (train_upstream +
  reason_agpt); `merge_lora_dcp_to_hf.py` folds LoRA-DCP for eval.

## 4. Big-mix SFT -- completed, then evaluated to a verdict

The full `tulu_math_uc_mix` (OpenMathInstruct-2 variant, ~54B tokens, 1 epoch)
finished cleanly at 8N (step 8672, epoch 1.0, loss 0.357) across a 10-link
fault-tolerant afterany chain. But the **eval verdict flips the naive reading**:

- **ckpt-8672 catastrophically forgot** -- every base-LM task collapsed to
  ~chance (hellaswag 0.59 -> 0.27, arc_easy 0.69 -> 0.30) between step ~1500-4500,
  and IFEval went flat-to-down.
- **checkpoint-900 (pre-collapse) is the deliverable**: retains base-LM
  (hellaswag 0.59, arc_easy 0.64), IFEval prompt-strict **0.253** (>> baseline
  0.179), and is a strong GRPO starting point (sum_digits accuracy_reward
  0.31 -> 0.74 in ~20 steps).
- **Root cause**: LR 2e-5 held >1e-5 through step ~4350 (cosine decay only bites
  the 2nd half); ~4000 steps at peak LR on the narrow math-CoT distribution
  overfit + forgot. The prior metamathqa recipe survived only by *stopping* at
  729 steps. **Lesson: cap full-mix SFT at O(1000) steps or lower the LR -- a
  full epoch at peak LR is the mistake, not the mix.**
  [`tulu_math_uc_mix_full/evals/README.md`](../../production/sft/agpt/2b-mds/tulu_math_uc_mix_full/evals/README.md).

## 5. 80B production NaN -- root-caused, doc corrected

The 80B NaN (dim=9216) at production data-parallel scale is a **bf16
forward-activation overflow in the 84-layer pre-norm residual stream, NOT an
optimizer bug** -- the `80b/README.md` banner's "SophiaG Hessian grad*grad
overflow" framing was wrong and was corrected across the docs.

- **Optimizer-independent**: mano (no Hessian term) @ 62N and SophiaG @ 510N NaN
  identically -- flat grad_norm ~6.0 for 13-16 steps, then sudden inf/nan with no
  runup. Loss softmax and grad reduction both ruled out (CE upcasts to fp32; FSDP
  reduce_dtype is fp32; loss is dp-invariant).
- **Mechanism**: `h = x + sublayer(norm(x))` accumulates in bf16; RMSNorm
  rescales sublayer *inputs* but never the residual stream. Trigger corner:
  LBS>1 AND dp_degree (=NGPUS/TP) > ~186.
- **fp32-residual prototypes both INSUFFICIENT**: per-block fp32 (casts back to
  bf16 at each boundary) trained clean at 4N but still NaN'd at dp=192; a
  full-depth fp32 residual carried across all 84 layers *also* NaN'd at dp=192 --
  so the true overflow site is elsewhere (attention scores or another
  activation), localizable only by per-op numerics capture (wired, paused).
- **Only confirmed unblock**: `--training.mixed-precision-param=float32` at TP=4
  (~3-5x slower). A `--nan-abort-consecutive` guard was added to the autoretry
  scripts and worked in production (bailed a diverged run, reclaimed walltime).
  Status: dormant (no live 80B production).
  [`2026-07-14-80b-fp32-residual-fix`](../../experiments/agpt/aurora/2026-07-14-80b-fp32-residual-fix.md).

## 7. Checkpoint migration -- converter proven

The old-nested -> new-flat DCP **optim-statedict converter** (PyTorch #3623
fused/foreach) was proven: a converted step-39600 checkpoint full-state-resumes
on HEAD to loss 2.66 / grad_norm 0.55 (matching a native 2.74), momentum
preserved. This unblocks migrating the pinned production clones to HEAD -- convert
the LATEST checkpoint per chain, resume on HEAD; never git-pull a pinned clone
(that breaks resume). A full 4-link **2B-512 HEAD-migration dress rehearsal**
passed end-to-end.

## 9. Synthetic-summary data-gen POC

Built + validated an end-to-end pipeline to generate synthetic mid-training data
by summarizing existing gemma-tokenized olmo-mix shards (`experiments/ezpz/
synthetic/`): detok .bin -> text -> vLLM/HF summarize -> QC -> retok to drop-in
blendcorpus .bin. DeepHermes-3-Llama-3-3B won the model bake-off; a 1787-doc
pilot ran clean (rc=0, QC kept 99.8%). **Key finding: ~9x compression** (2.06M
source tok -> 229K summary tok), so the eval gate must compare equal-token AND
equal-doc budgets; a real run needs a much larger corpus + vLLM.
[`2026-07-11-summarize-olmo-mix-poc`](../../experiments/synthetic/aurora/2026-07-11-summarize-olmo-mix-poc.md).

## 10. Production ops -- constant LR + queue starvation

- **Both canonical 20B chains held at constant LR** (decay-ratio=0) per request,
  patched into both prod clones + the main-repo autoretry script; continuations
  pick it up at launch (nothing killed). Both chains were pre-decay anyway.
- **Queue starvation** dominated the window: Aurora ran genuinely node-scarce
  (~218 free vs the 256/512 the prod jobs need, plus daily 01:00-08:00
  reservations), so the big prod jobs sat `at_queue` for days. The **capacity
  queue** (16N, 168h walltime, 2 slots/user) became the reliable low-contention
  trickle -- a GAS-matched 16N bridge holds GBS bit-identical to the canonical
  chain and advances it while the big jobs wait. 20B-512 crept 6000 -> 6100 this
  way; a matching 20B-256 bridge was set up at window's end.
- Corrected a mislabeled record: job 8661913 was NOT a constant-LR fork (its own
  resolved config shows random-init warmup, decay_ratio=0.8) -- a from-scratch
  duplicate that silent-hung at step-400, not resumed.
